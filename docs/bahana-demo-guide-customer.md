# AI Personalization Demo — Try It Yourself

**Prepared by Devoteam for Bahana Sekuritas**

A working demo you can try from your own phone, over WhatsApp. It takes about
five minutes.

---

## Before you start — three things to know

**The customer data is entirely fictional.** Every "nasabah" in this demo is
synthetic, generated by us. No Bahana data of any kind has been used. The
portfolios, balances and holdings you will see belong to people who do not exist.

**This is a demo, not a product.** It exists to show how the personalization
works, so you can judge whether the approach is right before anything is built
for real.

**Nothing here is investment advice.** The assistant is deliberately built so it
cannot recommend a specific security. If you ask it what to buy, it will decline
and offer to connect you to a person — that behaviour is one of the things worth
testing.

---

## How to start

**1. Message this number on WhatsApp:**

# +1 629 284 3510

**2. Your first message must end with a tag in square brackets.**

The tag chooses which fictional customer you want to be. For example:

```
Saham apa saja yang saya punya? [moderat]
```

That's the only rule. After the first tagged message you can chat normally —
you stay as that customer until you send a different tag.

You can write in Bahasa Indonesia or English; the assistant replies in whichever
language you use.

> **If you forget the tag**, the assistant will treat you as a brand-new customer
> it knows nothing about, and its answers will be generic. Send any question with
> a tag on the end and it will pick up the profile immediately.

---

## The three customers you can be

Each tag puts you in a different investor's shoes. They differ in risk profile,
what they own, and how long since they last traded — which is exactly what the
assistant's answers should reflect.

| Tag | Who you become | Cash in RDN | Currently holds | Last transaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[moderat]` | Budi Santoso — balanced investor | Rp 46.000.000 | BBCA, BBRI, TLKM | 190 days ago |
| `[konservatif]` | Sari Wijaya — cautious, cash sitting idle | Rp 82.500.000 | nothing yet | 312 days ago |
| `[agresif]` | Rizki Pratama — active trader | Rp 240.000.000 | ANTM, BBRI, ICBP, PGAS | 3 days ago |

Switch between them at any time by putting a different tag at the end of a
message. Several people can test at once — each phone gets its own separate
conversation.

## What is deliberately **not** in this demo

We would rather tell you than have you find out.

**No identity check.** The demo shows balances and holdings to whoever sends the
message. In a real deployment, specific figures would be released only after the
customer is verified — for example by OTP, or from inside your authenticated
app. Unverified users would get general product information and no numbers.

**No connection to Bahana systems.** Nothing here reads or writes any Bahana
data. The customer records are ours and fictional.

**The assistant answers directly.** In this demo it replies to you without a
human in between. For a securities firm we would normally propose the opposite
by default — the assistant drafts, a licensed person reviews and sends. Both
modes are built; we set this one so you can try it without waiting on us.

**One-way for now.** The demo shows the conversation. The relationship-manager
view — a daily list of clients worth contacting, with a drafted message for each
— is designed but not built.
